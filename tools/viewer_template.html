Olympia School District: staffing, spending, and revenue over time

Shows what the district reported to the state, with context. The charts describe; readers draw their own conclusions. Working version, Nov. 2026. Prepared independently; not affiliated with any community group.

About this page. This viewer was generated with Claude. It covers a decade of data drawn from thousands of pages of public documentation. We have tried to minimize errors, but it is presented as is. It is meant as an aid to understanding the district’s financial history, points of departure and trends. Every source is listed in SOURCES.md, every grouping and calculation is logged in the Methods tab and DECISIONS_AND_INFERENCES.md, and the cleaned data are in the GitHub repository (github.com/EmersonCodeProjects/osd_financials_15_26_viewer). To report an error or ask for a correction, open an issue on GitHub.
How to read this page. Charts show values as reported unless a tag says otherwise. Every figure carries a tag, and every tag links to a numbered entry on the Methods tab. RAW as published  REGROUPED summed under our category map  CALCULATED arithmetic on reported values  INFERRED depends on a guess about what the data means  EXTERNAL someone else's figure, not verified by us.

Methods: every grouping, calculation, and assumption

Each entry says what we did, why, what else we could have done, and how much it could matter. Status tells you whether a source confirms it or we assumed it. Same text as DECISIONS_AND_INFERENCES.md.

Sources: OSPI F-196 actual revenues and expenditures (2015-16 to 2024-25); OSPI S-275 personnel reports, October snapshot (2015-16 to 2025-26); district enrollment dashboard; OSPI S-275 handbooks; Seattle-area CPI table (Sept. 2026); community spreadsheets where tagged EXTERNAL, including the community workbook “OSD Admin Data-For Sharing” compiled by Janel Bistrika (2026), used with credit. Cleaned data and scripts are in the repository. Names of individuals are never published.
